# 4.3 Vegetative Vigor

**Outcome:** You can build a chili's chassis — roots, stem, leaf factory — with stage-correct FPJ/FAA and biology drenches before you ask the plant for a turbocharged fruit load.

---

## Core

Vegetative vigor is building the chassis before installing the turbo.

A Carolina Reaper with a weak frame and a huge early flower dump is a sports car on bicycle wheels. **Vegetative growth** is the leaf-and-root expansion phase: canopy that will later feed pods, roots that will drink Florida weather, stems that will hold fruit without snapping in a storm. Nitrogen-forward tools belong here. Heat bragging belongs later.

IC SPICY's vegetative kit (CookBook-locked):

- **FPJ** — fermented growing tips; gentle vegetative tonic.
- **FAA** — fish amino acids; stronger nitrogen repair when biology is already awake.
- **Transplant IMO + LAB drench** — settle the rhizosphere after the move.
- **Maintenance foliar cocktail** — FPJ + OHN + BRV as the Tuesday spray once the shelf is stocked — not a daily firehose.

Practice timing on Jalapeño if you are new; apply the same discipline to Red Habanero and Reaper once the muscle memory is clean.

**One practical takeaway for tonight:** if you see flowers on a plant that still looks like a twig, do not celebrate — ask whether the chassis is done, then check whether you are still spraying vegetative tonics out of habit.

## 🌶️ Deeper Heat

### FPJ numbers

From the CookBook FPJ card:

- Dilution **1:500–1:1,000** (seedlings toward the weaker end). [VERIFY vs CookBook: fermented-plant-juice-fpj]
- Every **7–10 days** during vegetative growth. [VERIFY vs CookBook: fermented-plant-juice-fpj]

Pull back as clusters set — lingering vegetative pushes can delay pod fill on some cultivars (CookBook tip). Full method: [/cookbook/fermented-plant-juice-fpj](/cookbook/fermented-plant-juice-fpj) (and Masterclass 2.1 for the why).

### FAA numbers

From the CookBook FAA card:

- Begin about **1:1,200** for seedlings; vigorous adults sometimes accept **1:500**. [VERIFY vs CookBook: fish-amino-acid-faa]
- Every **10–14 days** during leafy growth phases. [VERIFY vs CookBook: fish-amino-acid-faa]

Separate heavy FAA days from heavy IMO liquid drenches so biology can process nitrogen gradually (CookBook tip).

### Culture that chemistry cannot fake

- **Root health:** transplant drench per [/cookbook/transplant-imo-lab-drench](/cookbook/transplant-imo-lab-drench); avoid waterlogging "to help growth."
- **Topping / structure:** optional on vigorous plants once established — goal is branching architecture, not bonsai torture.
- **Maintenance cocktail:** equal micro-doses of FPJ, OHN, and BRV at target dilution (often ~1:500 each on the card) — spot-test first: [/cookbook/maintenance-foliar-cocktail](/cookbook/maintenance-foliar-cocktail).

### Stage gate

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Soft new leaves, expanding canopy | FPJ / maintenance cocktail on schedule |
| Pale, hungry, biology already active | Consider FAA at seedling-safe dilution first |
| First flower clusters on a sturdy frame | Taper FPJ; prepare calcium / FFJ tools (Lesson 4.4) |
| Flowers on a skinny plant | Fix light, roots, and frame — do not just add more N |
