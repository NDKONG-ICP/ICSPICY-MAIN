# 4.5 Ripening & Peak Heat

**Outcome:** You can finish pods for quality and heat with observation-based seawater progression, fermented seawater when biology should ride along, and FFJ — while treating "peak capsaicin day" claims as verify-flagged, not gospel.

---

## Core

Ripening is the last mile of a road trip when you finally see the heat shimmer.

The chassis is built. Fruit is set. Now the pod loads sugars, color, and **capsaicinoids** — the spicy compounds that make a Reaper a Reaper — while you resist the urge to pick everything green because you are excited. Color breaks, firmness, variety cues on Pepperpedia, and a clean finish program beat a calendar superstition.

IC SPICY's ripening kit:

- **Ripening seawater progression** — step dilution from gentler toward stronger **only when scouting shows safe margins**.
- **FSW (fermented seawater)** — minerals plus a lactic/rice-wash biology fraction for humid finishing blocks.
- **FFJ** — keep reproductive-stage support through sizing and finish (still not FPJ).

Ghost, Apocalypse Scorpion, Reaper, and Sugar Rush Peach each have their own color and heat-band stories — read the guide before you brag about a number.

**One practical takeaway for tonight:** start seawater at the weak end of the CookBook progression, walk the row for leaf-margin burn, and only then tighten. Last-mile driving is steering, not flooring it.

## 🌶️ Deeper Heat

### Seawater progression (CookBook-locked)

The ripening seawater progression card coordinates dilution stepping from **1:30 toward 1:20** only when scouting shows safe margin burn levels. It pairs with FFJ and WCA rather than substituting soil tests. [VERIFY vs CookBook: ripening-seawater-progression]

Related clean-seawater card language: dilute start **1:30** for first ripening passes; tighten only with observation; commonly **1:20–1:30** during ripening. [VERIFY vs CookBook: diluted-seawater-minerals]

Application frequency on the progression card is **threshold-based — never calendar-only**. [VERIFY vs CookBook: ripening-seawater-progression]

### FSW

Fermented seawater marries diluted seawater with rice-wash / LAB biology and a small FPJ fraction so minerals arrive with active biology. CookBook notes preventive use every **10–14 days** during humid fruiting, with seawater base starting weak (ingredient line: diluted **1:30** first). [VERIFY vs CookBook: fermented-seawater-fsw]

Never spray drought-stressed plants at midday with salt programs (CookBook safety).

### FFJ on the finish

FFJ stays in the ripening window at **1:500–1:1,000** every **7–14 days** from bloom through ripening. [VERIFY vs CookBook: fermented-fruit-juice-ffj]

### Capsaicin timing — honesty check

Internet charts that swear "peak heat is day X after color break" are not a substitute for variety trials on *your* farm. Capsaicinoid accumulation depends on genetics, light, temperature, water stress, nutrition, and fruit position. [VERIFY] Any specific "peak heat day-count" claim needs a cited lab or controlled trial before IC SPICY treats it as protocol — until then, harvest for full variety color/firmness cues from Pepperpedia and taste/heat check a sample fruit rather than worshiping a calendar.

### Harvest quality

- Pick for intended use (fresh, dry, ferment, powder).
- Avoid anthracnose-soft fruit in the box — Florida humid finishes are a scouting sport.
- Log cultivar + harvest color stage + any seawater/FSW applications for traceability.
