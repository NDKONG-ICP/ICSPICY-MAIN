# 2.3 JADAM JMS & JLF

**Outcome:** You can explain when JADAM's thrift-store toolkit beats waiting on a months-long KNF shelf — and how to dilute JMS, JLF, and JWA from the IC SPICY CookBook without inventing cowboy ratios.

---

## Core

JADAM is the thrift-store toolkit that still builds a greenhouse.

KNF inputs (FPJ, OHN, IMO) are craft bottles — beautiful, slow, sometimes precious. JADAM, developed and taught by **Youngsang Cho**, answers a different question: *what if the farm needs microbes and fertility this week, from leaf mold, starch water, and weeds, for pocket change?* You are not choosing a religion. You are choosing a toolbox. IC SPICY keeps both on the truck. A Jalapeño practice row is a fine place to learn the thrift-store moves before you trust them on a Habanero block.

**JMS (JADAM Microorganism Solution)** is a fast microbial bloom — leaf mold or compost, starch water, non-chlorinated water, a pinch of sea salt, fermented in roughly **2–4 days**. Dilute hard: commonly **1:100 to 1:500**. Use weekly for about **3 weeks post-transplant**, then monthly. [VERIFY vs CookBook: jadam-microorganism-solution-jms] Smell should go malty/earthy; rotten eggs means anaerobic failure — dump and restart, do not "hope spray" it.

**JLF (JADAM Liquid Fertilizer)** is the green-manure brew: chopped cover-crop biomass, **2–5%** mature compost starter by mass, water to cover, **10–20 days** ferment. Start weak on sand — CookBook dilution notes often land in a **1:50–1:200** conversation depending on crop hunger and sap response. [VERIFY vs CookBook: jadam-liquid-fertilizer-jlf] It is fertility with biology, not a salt bottle with a friendly label.

**JWA (JADAM Wetting Agent)** is the spreader — roughly **≈1–2 mL per liter** of spray, last into the tank when you need oils, mineral suspensions, or stubborn leaf coverage. [VERIFY vs CookBook: jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]

Rice-wash capture is the free side quest that feeds LAB and seawater ferments too — same thrift-store energy, different jar. None of this replaces a stocked KNF shelf; it keeps the farm moving when craft bottles are empty or still aging.

**One practical takeaway for tonight:** if your KNF shelf is empty but transplants went in yesterday, JMS is the thrift-store jumpstart. If your shelf is full, JMS still earns a lane as a bridge between heavy ferments — not as a reason to throw away FPJ.

## 🌶️ Deeper Heat

### JADAM vs KNF — same farm, different clocks

| Need | Lean JADAM | Lean KNF |
|---|---|---|
| Microbes in 2–4 days | **JMS** | IMO sequence (weeks–months) |
| Cheap bulk fertility from biomass | **JLF** | FAA / compost teas / FFJ stage tools |
| Herbal vitality micro-dose | — | **OHN** |
| Vegetative tonic from tips | — | **FPJ** |
| Surfactant for sprays | **JWA** | Commercial wetters (if allowed) |

IC SPICY does not make you pick a jersey. We make you pick a **clock** and a **budget**.

### JMS — the bloom in a barrel

CookBook primary: [/cookbook/jadam-microorganism-solution-jms](/cookbook/jadam-microorganism-solution-jms)

- Ferment **2–4 days**; dilution **1:100–1:500**. [VERIFY vs CookBook: jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]
- Cadence: **weekly × 3 weeks** after transplant, then **monthly**. [VERIFY vs CookBook: jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]
- Feedstock honesty: local leaf mold / quality compost beats mystery powder. You are culturing *your* watershed's microbes, JADAM-style.

Use JMS to inoculate tired sand, bridge after stress, or keep biology moving when IMO4 is not ready. Do not use it as a pesticide fantasy.

### JLF — fertility from the weedy aisle

CookBook: [/cookbook/jadam-liquid-fertilizer-jlf](/cookbook/jadam-liquid-fertilizer-jlf)

- Compost starter **2–5%** of mass; ferment **10–20 days**. [VERIFY vs CookBook: jadam-liquid-fertilizer-jlf]
- Application rate is **highly variable — start weak** on sandier soils; weekly on hungry rotations, back off when plants look overfed. [VERIFY vs CookBook: jadam-liquid-fertilizer-jlf]
- Dilution discipline in nursery practice often starts in the **1:50–1:200** range and adjusts by observation — confirm against the CookBook page for your tank math. [VERIFY vs CookBook: jadam-liquid-fertilizer-jlf]

Rotate green-manure species year to year. Night irrigation reduces phototoxicity when particulates remain.

### JWA — the last ingredient

CookBook: [/cookbook/jadam-wetting-agent-jwa](/cookbook/jadam-wetting-agent-jwa)

- **≈1–2 mL/L** spray solution. [VERIFY vs CookBook: jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]
- KOH + oil soap-making has real safety rules (no aluminum, PPE). Read the page before you play chemist.
- Hard well water may need pretreatment — foam-test in a jar first.

### Rice wash — the free starter culture

Capturing rice-wash water is the humble cousin that shows up in LAB and seawater ferments too:

→ [/cookbook/rice-wash-water-capture](/cookbook/rice-wash-water-capture)

Do not pour chlorinated kitchen sludge into a carefully built JMS barrel. Non-chlorinated, logged, and used on purpose.

### Failure modes

- Sealed anaerobic JMS → egg smell → discard.
- JLF too strong on sand → soft growth, salt stress vibes without the salt bag.
- JWA first into a weird tank-mix → break emulsions; add last.
- Calling JADAM "the same as IMO" — related goal, different method and timeline.
