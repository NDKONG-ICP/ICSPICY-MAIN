# 2.4 Calcium & Mineral Inputs

**Outcome:** You can stage calcium and mineral tools for flowering and fruit swell — WCA, WCP, seawater, and bone char — and explain why blossom-end rot is often a delivery problem, not a "buy a bigger calcium bottle" problem.

---

## Core

Calcium is the brick mortar for expanding fruit cells.

When a pepper pod swells fast in Florida heat, new cell walls need calcium delivered on time through the transpiration stream. If irrigation swings wild, humidity stalls movement, or the plant is pushing too many pods on a weak root system, the distal end of the fruit runs out of mortar. That ugly sunken patch — blossom-end rot — is often **logistics**, not a simple soil-test deficiency. You can have calcium in the ground and still starve the tip of the fruit.

KNF's answer is gentle, foliar-available calcium from eggshells and vinegar (**WCA**), plus a phosphorus-leaning sibling from burned bone (**WCP**), timed to flowering and pod expansion — not dumped weekly from seedling day like a superstition. Superhots that set heavy flushes are especially good teachers: the plant will show you whether mortar arrived on schedule.

**WCA** — water-soluble calcium: eggshells prepared about **1:10** with vinegar, then foliar at **1:1,000**, every **7–10 days** during rapid pod expansion. [VERIFY vs CookBook: water-soluble-calcium-wca]

**WCP** — water-soluble calcium phosphate: foliar baseline **1:1,000**, every **10–14 days** during flowering and early fruit. [VERIFY vs CookBook: water-soluble-calcium-phosphate-wcp]

**Seawater minerals** — ripening lane, diluted **1:20–1:30**, not a seedling shower. [VERIFY vs CookBook: diluted-seawater-minerals]

IC SPICY's recipes and the blossom-end rot protocol are the source of truth — this lesson is the *why and when* so you stop spraying calcium at the wrong life stage and calling it a personality. Bone char belongs in the slow mineral furniture conversation; seawater belongs at finish. Keep the jars labeled like harvest depends on it — because it does.

**One practical takeaway for tonight:** fix irrigation uniformity and stage timing before you triple the WCA. Mortar trucks do not help if the road to the fruit is closed.

## 🌶️ Deeper Heat

### Stage map (where minerals earn rent)

| Stage | Goal | Calcium / mineral lean |
|---|---|---|
| Seedling / early veg | Roots + leaf | Go easy; seed priming is a separate CookBook tool, not weekly WCA spam |
| Pre-bloom / flower | Set the table | **WCP** enters; taper pure vegetative tonics |
| Pod expansion | Cell wall integrity | **WCA** on the 7–10 day rhythm |
| Ripening / finish | Brix, trace minerals | **Diluted seawater** / **FSW**; watch salinity |

FPJ was the vegetative chapter. This is the reproductive chapter. Wrong-chapter spraying is how you get pretty leaves and corky fruit.

### WCA — eggshell mortar

Primary: [/cookbook/water-soluble-calcium-wca](/cookbook/water-soluble-calcium-wca)

- Prep: clean roasted shells with vinegar at about **1:10**; never seal a reacting jar tight (CO₂). [VERIFY vs CookBook: water-soluble-calcium-wca]
- Apply: **1:1,000** foliar; **7–10 days** during pod expansion. [VERIFY vs CookBook: water-soluble-calcium-wca]
- Timing trick: dusk after peak transpiration stress often beats midday heroics.

### WCP — flower power, not a P dump

Primary: [/cookbook/water-soluble-calcium-phosphate-wcp](/cookbook/water-soluble-calcium-phosphate-wcp)

- **1:1,000** foliar baseline; **every 10–14 days** flowering / early fruit. [VERIFY vs CookBook: water-soluble-calcium-phosphate-wcp]
- Complements WCA; does not replace a real soil P diagnosis if deficiency is extreme.
- Bone-burning has PPE and neighbor-courtesy rules — read the safety notes.

### Blossom-end rot — the protocol, not the myth

→ [/cookbook/blossom-end-rot-prevention](/cookbook/blossom-end-rot-prevention)

BER prevention integrates **WCA timing**, **irrigation uniformity**, and **transpiration management** so calcium reaches distal fruits. Soil sufficiency alone is not the whole story. Scout, log, and change one variable at a time — usually water first.

### Seawater & fermented seawater

- **Diluted seawater:** [/cookbook/diluted-seawater-minerals](/cookbook/diluted-seawater-minerals) — commonly **1:20–1:30** during ripening; every **10–14 days** through final swell; never jump concentrations overnight. [VERIFY vs CookBook: diluted-seawater-minerals]
- **FSW:** [/cookbook/fermented-seawater-fsw](/cookbook/fermented-seawater-fsw) — diluted seawater plus rice-wash/LAB biology and a trace FPJ fraction for humid finishing blocks; same salinity caution as plain seawater.

Coastal Florida growers: sample clean water away from discharge; respect red-tide advisories. Undiluted seawater is not "more minerals." It is a burn.

### Bone char — slow mineral furniture

→ [/cookbook/bone-char-mineral-adjustment](/cookbook/bone-char-mineral-adjustment)

Bone char / char-phosphate pucks are controlled-release mineral sites in carbon pores — multi-season furniture, not a foliar emergency. Use when soil tests and rotation plans say P/mineral banks need structure, not when a single fruit looks sad today.

### Failure modes

- Daily calcium sprays from week one → wasted effort, possible leaf junk.
- Seawater on drought-stressed plants at noon → salt burn.
- Ignoring drip emitter clogs / wet-dry extremes → BER returns despite perfect WCA.
- Grabbing the WCA jug when you meant BRV because both look like "brown liquid in a reused bottle" — label like your harvest depends on it.

### Attribution

Eggshell calcium, seawater mineral use, and related KNF mineral tools sit in the broader **Master Cho / Natural Farming** input family as practiced and taught by many instructors. JADAM and extension literature also discuss salinity and calcium disorders. IC SPICY CookBook pages are the procedural source here; Further Reading credits lineages without scraping course text.

**In-game:** Pepper Patch — phase-correct feeding at flowering. WCA/WCP in the wrong phase should feel like a wasted craft; right phase should support pod quality.

**Garden assignment:** Pick one flowering/early-fruit plant. (1) Check irrigation: even moisture, no wild swings for a week. (2) Mix WCA at **1:1,000** per CookBook and apply at dusk. (3) Log fruit set notes for 10 days. If BER already present, follow the blossom-end-rot-prevention page — do not just double the dose. Optional ripening block: one seawater pass at **1:20–1:30** on mature pods only.
