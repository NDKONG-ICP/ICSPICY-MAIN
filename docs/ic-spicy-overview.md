# What is IC SPICY?

IC SPICY is a real-world-asset (RWA) e-commerce application on the Internet Computer at https://icspicy.app. It connects a FDACS-registered pepper nursery in Port Charlotte, Florida with blockchain-verified plant provenance, a curated shop, and community tools for pepper growers worldwide.

## How It Works

Every plant grown at the IC SPICY nursery can carry an ICRC-7 NFT from the 8,888-token "IC SPICY" collection. The NFT is not just art — it records the plant's full lifecycle through NIMS (Nursery Inventory Management System), from germination through sale and beyond. When you buy a plant, the NFT transfers to your wallet as a verifiable certificate of authenticity and care history.

The app runs entirely on-chain canisters: a backend for inventory, orders, and plant data; an NFT asset canister for collection metadata and images; and SpicyAI, a knowledge-base chatbot trained on IC SPICY documentation and natural-farming guides.

## FDACS Registration

IC SPICY operates as a registered nursery under the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services (FDACS). Plants sold through the shop are grown at the Port Charlotte facility and ship with the regulatory compliance appropriate for live plant sales. The on-chain provenance layer adds transparency beyond what traditional nurseries offer — buyers can see exactly how their plant was raised.

## The NFT Collection

The IC SPICY collection contains 8,888 ICRC-7 tokens across rarity tiers (Common through Legendary), plus 888 PepperHead membership NFTs. Plant-purchased NFTs are assigned automatically when nursery seeds germinate in NIMS. PepperHeads are a separate membership tier with community benefits, discounts, and premium SpicyAI access — they are not tied to a physical plant purchase.

## Who Is It For?

- **Home gardeners** — free NIMS plant tracking, weather tools, seed bank, and SpicyAI growing advice
- **Collectors** — verified provenance for rare pepper varieties with on-chain lifecycle data
- **Customers** — buy live plants with crypto (ICP, ckBTC) or card payments via Stripe/ICPay
- **Community members** — PepperHead membership, OHSHII governance links, and premium chatbot tiers
