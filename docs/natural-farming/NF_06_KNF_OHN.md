# KNF Oriental Herbal Nutrient (OHN)
**How to make the herbal immune-booster that goes into every KNF application — the backbone of plant resilience.**
*For growers building crop immunity against pests, disease, and environmental stress.*

---

Oriental Herbal Nutrient (OHN) is a fermented extract of five herbs, preserved in alcohol and brown sugar. It is the most versatile input in Korean Natural Farming. OHN is added to nearly every spray — it works as a prebiotic that encourages beneficial aerobic microbes in the soil and on plant surfaces, while discouraging anaerobic pathogens. It also functions as an insect repellent and stress protector.

Master Cho described OHN as medicine for the plant: "full of energy and function to increase plant robustness, to sterilize, to keep plants warm, and to revitalize crops and activating their growth."

OHN is the most time-consuming KNF input to make, but one batch lasts years. Making it is a one-time investment.

## The Five Herbs

**Fresh herbs (raw state):**
- Garlic (*Allium sativum*) — antibacterial, antifungal
- Ginger (*Zingiber officinale*) or Turmeric (*Curcuma longa*)

**Dried herbs (rehydrated):**
- Angelica (*Angelica acutiloba* / *Angelica gigans*) — main herb, 2× quantity
- Licorice root (*Glycyrrhiza uralensis* or *Glycyrrhiza glabra*)
- Cinnamon bark (*Cinnamomum* sp.)

**Proportions in the final OHN blend (CGNF official):**
2 parts angelica : 1 part licorice : 1 part cinnamon : 1 part ginger or turmeric : 1 part garlic

All five herbs are available from Asian grocery stores, herb suppliers, or online.

## How to Make OHN

OHN is made by preparing each herb extract separately, then blending them.

**Materials per herb:**
- Separate glass jars (one per herb; two for angelica)
- Rice wine or beer (for rehydrating dried herbs)
- Raw brown sugar
- Vodka or other 40% proof liquor (acts as preservative and solvent)
- Wooden spoon
- Muslin cloth or paper towels
- Rubber bands or mason jar rings

**Prep for Fresh Herbs (garlic, ginger, turmeric):**
1. Slice or crush fresh ginger or turmeric; slice or crush garlic. Each in its own jar.
2. Add equal weight brown sugar to each jar.
3. Cover with muslin/paper, secure with rubber band.
4. Let sit 5–7 days at room temperature out of direct sunlight.
5. Add vodka to fill jar completely.
6. Replace cover. Stir clockwise with wooden spoon every morning for **14 days**.
7. Strain 1/3 of the liquid from each jar into separate labeled extract jars.
8. Repeat steps 5–7 up to 5 more times (each extraction adds volume to the extract jars).

**Prep for Dried Herbs (angelica, licorice, cinnamon):**
1. Chop each dried herb into ½-inch cubes. Place each in its own jar, filling 1/3 full.
2. Add rice wine or beer to bring contents to ½ full. Let sit 2 days to rehydrate.
3. Add brown sugar to bring contents to 2/3 full. Let sit 5–7 days.
4. Add vodka to fill the jar. Replace cover.
5. Stir clockwise daily with wooden spoon for 14 days.
6. Strain 1/3 into separate labeled extract jars.
7. Repeat extractions 3–5 more times.

**Blending into OHN:**
1. Combine individual herb extracts in proportions: 2 parts angelica : 1 part each of licorice, cinnamon, ginger/turmeric, garlic.
2. Stir lightly with wooden spoon.
3. Cover loosely (allow air circulation — gases will form).
4. Store in a cool, dark cabinet for 6–12 months.
5. Always shake before use.

**Preparation season:** Spring or fall is ideal (23–25°C / 73–77°F). Storage temperature: 1–15°C (34–59°F).

## How to Use OHN

**Standard dilution: 1:1,000**

| Water Volume | Kitchen Measure | mL |
|---|---|---|
| ¼ gallon | ⅓ tsp | 2 |
| 1 gallon | ¾ tsp | 4 |
| 5 gallons | 1¼ Tbsp | 19 |
| 10 gallons | 2½ Tbsp | 38 |
| 25 gallons | just under ½ cup | 95 |
| 50 gallons | just over ¾ cup | 189 |

Dilute to 1:1,500 if applying to stressed plants or during drought.

**As foliar spray:**
- Combine: 2 parts OHN-angelica + 1 part each of other OHN extracts, diluted 1:1,000 total
- Apply as a cocktail with FPJ and BRV every 10–12 days to reduce susceptibility to powdery and downy mildew
- Apply in late afternoon or early morning
- OHN is used throughout the entire growth cycle — it does not change with growth stage

**As soil drench:**
- Dilute to 1:1,000 and apply before planting to activate dormant soil microorganisms

**For IMO and seed treatment:**
- OHN is a component of IMO2 and IMO4 preparations
- Add to seed-soak solutions with WCA and LAB

**In livestock production:**
- Mix at 1:1,000 into drinking water 3× weekly as a prebiotic
