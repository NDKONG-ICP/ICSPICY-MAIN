# KNF Complete Application Guide
**How to combine and schedule all KNF inputs across the full plant growth cycle — a practical spray calendar.**
*For growers ready to run a complete natural farming program on their garden or farm.*

---

This guide brings together all nine KNF inputs into a practical application schedule, organized by growth stage. Every recipe uses the standard CGNF dilution rates from the Hawaii Natural Farming network. Adjust timing based on plant response, weather, and observation.

The principle behind KNF scheduling is simple: **read the plant, not the calendar**. These timelines are guides. An actively growing plant may need more frequent applications; a stressed plant may need weaker dilutions.

## Input Quick Reference

| Input | Abbr | Standard Dilution | Growth Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indigenous Microorganisms | IMO | 1:500–1:1,000 | All stages |
| Lactic Acid Bacteria | LAB | 1:1,000 | Vegetative, soil prep |
| Fermented Plant Juice | FPJ | 1:500–1:1,000 | Vegetative |
| Fermented Fruit Juice | FFJ | 1:500–1:1,000 | Reproductive |
| Brown Rice Vinegar | BRV | 1:1,000 | All stages (cocktail) |
| Oriental Herbal Nutrient | OHN | 1:1,000 | All stages |
| Fish Amino Acid | FAA | 1:1,000 | Vegetative only |
| Water-Soluble Calcium | WCA | 1:1,000 | Reproductive |
| Water-Soluble Calcium Phosphate | WCP | 1:1,000 | Reproductive |
| Seawater | SEA | 1:30 | Ripening |

## Stage 1: Soil Preparation (Before Planting)

**Goals:** Establish microbial community, activate soil biology, prepare seed bed.

| Application | Inputs | Dilution | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soil drench | IMO (liquid) | 1:500 | 100 gal/acre or 1 gal/10 sq ft |
| Soil drench | LAB | 1:1,000 | Mix into IMO drench |
| Soil drench | OHN | 1:1,000 | Mix into IMO drench |
| Top dressing | IMO4 (solid) | undiluted | 1,200 lb/acre or 1 cup per plant |

Apply 1–2 weeks before transplanting or seeding. Water in well.

**Seed soak (night before planting):**
Mix in 1 quart water: ¾ tsp WCA + ¾ tsp OHN + ¾ tsp LAB. Soak seeds for 8–12 hours. This improves germination and pre-inocultes seeds with beneficial microbes.

---

## Stage 2: Vegetative / Nutritive Growth

**Goals:** Build leaf area, strong root system, vigorous stems. Maximize plant size before flipping to reproductive.

**Weekly or every-10-day spray (the backbone cocktail):**
- Per gallon water: ¾ tsp FPJ + ¾ tsp OHN + ¾ tsp BRV
- Apply as foliar spray in the early morning or evening

**Supplemental nitrogen (every 10–14 days):**
- Per gallon water: ¾ tsp FAA
- Apply as foliar mist or soil drench
- *Stop FAA when flower buds first appear*

**Monthly soil drench:**
- Per gallon water: ¾ tsp IMO + ¾ tsp LAB
- Apply around root zone
- Builds and maintains soil microbial community

**Signs of correct vegetative-stage nutrition:**
- Dark green, glossy leaves
- Thick, strong stems
- Rapid leaf production
- No yellowing of lower leaves

---

## Stage 3: Change-Over / Transition

**Goals:** Signal the plant to shift energy from leaves to flowers and fruit. This is the pivot point.

**At the first sign of flower buds:**
1. **Stop all FAA immediately.** Nitrogen at this point pushes vegetative growth and delays fruiting.
2. **Stop or reduce FPJ.** Switch from FPJ to FFJ.
3. **Begin WCA sprays.**

**Transition cocktail (every 7 days):**
- Per gallon water: ¾ tsp WCA + ¾ tsp OHN + ¾ tsp FFJ
- Apply as foliar mist in evening

---

## Stage 4: Reproductive / Fruiting

**Goals:** Strong flower set, fruit development without blossom-end rot, building sugar.

**Every 7–10 days mineral spray:**
- Per gallon water: ¾ tsp WCA + ¾ tsp WCP + ¾ tsp OHN
- Apply in evening or early morning — never during hot midday sun

**Every 10–14 days immune spray (mildew/disease prevention):**
- Per gallon water: ¾ tsp OHN + ¾ tsp BRV + ¾ tsp FFJ

**For Florida anthracnose prevention (humid season only):**
- Apply fermented seawater spray every 10–14 days during rainy season

---

## Stage 5: Ripening

**Goals:** Maximize sugar, color, and capsaicin development. Bring fruit to peak expression.

**Ripening seawater spray (every 7–10 days):**
- Seawater at 1:30 dilution — early ripening
- Seawater at 1:25 — mid ripening
- Seawater at 1:20 — near harvest
- Add OHN at ¾ tsp per gallon to each seawater spray

**Signs of correct ripening support:**
- Deep, vibrant color
- Firm flesh
- Intense fragrance
- Smooth, non-pitted skin

## Weekly Spray Calendar (Simplified)

| Week | Spray A | Spray B (optional) |
|---|---|---|
| Soil prep | IMO + LAB + OHN drench | IMO4 top-dress |
| 1–2 (seedling) | FPJ + OHN + BRV (1:500) | Seed soak: WCA+OHN+LAB |
| 3–6 (vegetative) | FPJ + OHN + BRV | FAA every 2 weeks |
| Bud break | WCA + OHN + FFJ | Stop FAA |
| Flowering | WCA + WCP + OHN | BRV + OHN (disease) |
| Fruiting | WCA + WCP + OHN | Fermented seawater (humidity) |
| Ripening | Seawater + OHN | FFJ |

## Spray Timing Tips

- **Never spray in full sun or temperatures above 90°F** — solutions evaporate before absorption and may cause leaf burn.
- **Best windows:** 30 minutes after sunrise, or 1 hour before sunset. Florida's summer afternoons are too hot.
- **Rainy days:** Skip foliar sprays; rain will wash inputs off. Apply soil drenches instead.
- **After transplanting:** Wait 3–5 days before foliar spraying. Let the plant establish. Give an IMO soil drench at transplant time.
