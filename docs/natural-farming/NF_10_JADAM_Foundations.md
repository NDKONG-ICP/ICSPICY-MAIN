# JADAM Organic Farming: Foundations
**The ultra-low-cost farming system developed by Youngsang Cho — and how it differs from KNF.**
*For growers comparing natural farming methods and exploring JADAM's simplified pesticide and fertilizer approach.*

---

JADAM (야담, Ya-Dam) Organic Farming was developed by Youngsang Cho, the son of Master Han-Kyu Cho (founder of KNF). Despite the family connection, JADAM is a distinct system with different priorities. Where KNF focuses on plant growth stage matching and complex fermented inputs, JADAM focuses on radical cost reduction — making effective organic farming accessible to subsistence farmers and small operations worldwide.

Youngsang Cho's stated goal: "Lower the cost of farming to $100 per acre or less."

JADAM's major technical contributions are: JADAM Sulfur (JS) for fungal control, JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA) for spray penetration, JADAM Microorganism Solution (JMS) for soil biology, and JADAM Herbal Solution (JHS) for pest control.

## Key Philosophy Differences: KNF vs JADAM

| Dimension | KNF (Master Han-Kyu Cho) | JADAM (Youngsang Cho) |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Plant growth stage nutrition | Ultra-low cost pest and disease control |
| Soil building | IMO, LAB, FPJ, FAA — detailed inputs | JMS — single simple microbial input |
| Pest/disease | OHN as preventive | JS (fungicide), JHS (pesticide), JWA (wetter) |
| Complexity | High (9+ inputs, strict staging) | Low (few inputs, simple recipes) |
| Core ingredient | Brown sugar + local plants | Leaves, soft water, potato starch |
| Time to make | Weeks to months | Days to hours |
| USDA organic? | Mostly yes | Explicitly yes — Youngsang Cho verifies |
| Teaching style | Formal training, institute | Open-source, free on jadam.kr |

Both systems are compatible and many farmers use them together: KNF for soil fertility and plant nutrition, JADAM for pest and disease control.

## JADAM's Core Inputs

| Input | Abbreviation | Function |
|---|---|---|
| JADAM Microorganism Solution | JMS | Soil biology builder (simplified IMO) |
| JADAM Sulfur | JS | Organic fungicide (replaces lime-sulfur) |
| JADAM Wetting Agent | JWA | Surfactant — makes all sprays more effective |
| JADAM Herbal Solution | JHS | Pest deterrent and contact pesticide |
| JADAM Microbial Pesticide | JMP | Living pathogen-specific biological control |

## Water Quality Is Critical in JADAM

Youngsang Cho emphasizes that **soft water** is essential for JADAM spray preparations. Hard water (high calcium and magnesium mineral content) reacts with sulfur and soap-based wetting agents, forming insoluble precipitates that reduce effectiveness.

**The JADAM water test:** Pour JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA) into a glass of water. If it foams easily and the foam holds, the water is soft enough. If the foam quickly collapses, the water is too hard.

Solutions to hard water:
- Collect rainwater (naturally soft)
- Use a water softener
- Add a small amount of JWA to the water first, then add JS or JHS

In Florida: City water in South Florida (Miami-Dade) is moderately hard (100–200 ppm hardness). Rainwater collection is the simplest JADAM solution. Filtration through activated charcoal also helps.

## JADAM Is Open-Source

Youngsang Cho explicitly did not patent JADAM Sulfur or other JADAM methods, stating: "Mr. Cho did not patent the knowledge in order to share it with the farmers of the world." Full recipes and instructional videos are available free at en.jadam.kr.

His books — particularly *Ultra-Low-Cost Organic Farming* and *JADAM Organic Pest and Disease Control* — are the primary English-language references.
