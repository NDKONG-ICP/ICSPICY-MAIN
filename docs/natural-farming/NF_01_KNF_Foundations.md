# Korean Natural Farming: Foundations
**The philosophy, inputs, and growth-stage framework developed by Master Cho Han-Kyu.**
*For farmers, gardeners, and anyone growing food using biological rather than chemical methods.*

---

Korean Natural Farming (KNF) was developed by Master Han-Kyu Cho in Korea and spread worldwide through his Cho Global Natural Farming (CGNF) institute. The method combines ancient Asian farming wisdom with modern understanding of soil microbiology. It is practiced in over 20 countries and is ISO-certified for environmental restoration and healthful produce.

## Core Philosophy

KNF operates on a simple principle: give plants what they need at each growth stage, using inputs made from locally available materials. The farmer's job is to assist nature, not override it. Every input in KNF is fermented or cultured from farm waste, kitchen scraps, or the local environment. This makes it accessible and low-cost.

Master Cho taught that soil should host a balanced community of microorganisms — not dominated by bacteria alone (as in bare soil) and not by fungi alone (as in deep forest), but a balanced microbial ecosystem in the middle, which is where most food crops thrive. Indigenous Microorganisms (IMO) build that balance.

Chris Trump, a Master Natural Farmer certified by Master Cho who studied under him for 12 years, emphasizes that KNF is scaleable from a container on a balcony to 800-acre commercial farms. The methods work at any scale.

## The Nine Core Inputs

| Input | Abbreviation | Role in KNF |
|---|---|---|
| Indigenous Microorganisms | IMO | Foundation — builds soil biology |
| Lactic Acid Bacteria | LAB | Police — controls pathogens, aids decomp |
| Fermented Plant Juice | FPJ | Food — vegetative-stage nutrients |
| Fermented Fruit Juice | FFJ | Food (ripening stage) |
| Brown Rice Vinegar | BRV | Cleanser — balances pH, fungal control |
| Oriental Herbal Nutrient | OHN | Medicine — stress resilience, immune boost |
| Fish Amino Acid | FAA | Fuel — nitrogen during vegetative growth |
| Water-Soluble Calcium | WCA | Structure — cell walls, blossom-end rot prevention |
| Water-Soluble Calcium Phosphate | WCP | Reproduction — flowering and fruiting |
| Seawater / Fermented Seawater | SEA | Minerals — ripening, sugar content |

## Growth Stage Framework

KNF matches inputs to plant growth stages. Applying the wrong input at the wrong stage is counterproductive (e.g., FAA nitrogen during fruiting delays ripening).

**Vegetative / Nutritive Growth Stage**
- Goal: build leaf mass and strong root system
- Inputs: IMO (soil drench), FPJ (foliar), FAA (foliar or drench), OHN (foliar), LAB (soil drench)
- Standard maintenance mix: FPJ + OHN + BRV at 1:500 dilution, applied every 7–10 days

**Change-Over / Transition Stage**
- Goal: signal transition from vegetative to reproductive growth
- Stop FAA (nitrogen pushes leafy growth, delays flowering)
- Switch to WCA (calcium supports cell division for fruit set)
- OHN continues as stress protection

**Reproductive / Fruiting Stage**
- Goal: support fruit development, prevent blossom-end rot, build sugar
- Inputs: WCA (foliar), WCP (foliar), OHN, SEA (to boost brix/sugar), FFJ
- Apply WCA at 1:1,000 in the evening or early morning to avoid leaf burn

## The Maintenance Spray Formula

A simplified daily-use formula used by Natural Farming Hawaii practitioners:
- 1 part OHN + 2 parts BRV + 2 parts FPJ
- Dilute combined at 20 mL per gallon (approximately 1:500 to 1:800 total)
- Spray weekly or every 10–12 days

This mix supports soil microbes, deters foliar pathogens, and provides vegetative-stage nutrition in one application. Adjust based on plant response.
