# IC SPICY Retail Readiness Checklist

**What must be ready before serious grocery outreach.**

*For CPG operations and buyer conversations.*

> IC SPICY branding: dark luxury agriculture, fire-red accents, gold highlights, and the tagline "Rare. Hot. Alive."

> Draft marketing material. Legal review required before public token, investment, or regulatory claims.

---

## Product

Final recipes, stable batch process, ingredient sourcing, shelf life, packaging, labels, net weight, UPCs, case packs, and quality checks.

## Business

Wholesale pricing, MSRP, margin model, opening order terms, reorder terms, lead times, freight policy, insurance, W-9/entity info, and invoicing process.

## Compliance

Nutrition facts if required, ingredient/allergen review, claims review, food safety process, local/state requirements, and any needed facility or co-packer documentation.

## Sales

Sell sheet, line sheet, sample kit, demo plan, product photos, buyer email, retail launch calendar, and reorder tracking sheet.

## Proof

DTC sales, reviews, demo conversion, local account reorders, social engagement, and units per store per week.
