# IC SPICY Email Launch Sequence

**Email copy structure for waitlist and drop campaigns.**

*For Mailchimp, Beehiiv, ConvertKit, or manual email sends.*

> IC SPICY branding: dark luxury agriculture, fire-red accents, gold highlights, and the tagline "Rare. Hot. Alive."

> Draft marketing material. Legal review required before public token, investment, or regulatory claims.

---

## Email 1 - Welcome

Subject: Welcome to IC SPICY. Body: founder story, rare peppers, chef-built flavor, and what subscribers will get first.

## Email 2 - The Mission

Subject: From soil to shelf. Body: explain the grocery-shelf ambition, why the category needs origin, and how customers can help early.

## Email 3 - The First Blends

Subject: Meet the first four fires. Body: introduce each SKU with tasting notes and use cases.

## Email 4 - Provenance

Subject: Why the QR matters. Body: explain traceability, plant records, and ICP in simple customer language.

## Email 5 - Drop Day

Subject: The first IC SPICY batch is live. Body: product link, limited quantity, recipe idea, and share CTA.
