# NIMS — Nursery Inventory Management System

## What is NIMS?

NIMS (Nursery Inventory Management System) is IC SPICY's free plant tracking utility built into the IC SPICY application at https://icspicy.app. NIMS allows any user — from home gardeners to professional nursery owners — to track their entire plant collection with detailed lifecycle data, weather context, and feeding schedules.

For IC SPICY's FDACS-registered nursery in Port Charlotte, Florida, NIMS is the backbone of the Real World Asset (RWA) NFT provenance system. Every plant in the nursery inventory is assigned an ICRC-7 NFT on the Internet Computer blockchain. The NFT records the complete lifecycle of the plant — from seed to sale — creating a verifiable provenance certificate that travels with the plant when it's sold.

NIMS is NOT the same as the Nutrient Input Management System from Korean Natural Farming. IC SPICY's NIMS is a digital plant inventory and lifecycle tracking tool.

### Home Gardeners
- Track all your plants: peppers, tomatoes, herbs, anything
- Log watering, feeding, pest observations, and photos
- Monitor local weather automatically via GPS
- Access planting recommendations for your zone

### Nursery Owners (Admin)
- Full inventory management with NFT integration
- 72-cell germination tray tracking with visual grid

### Plant Buyers (Customers)

- All previous lifecycle data (from the nursery) is visible
- Continue logging your own watering, feeding, and observations
- The plant's complete story — from seed to your garden — lives on the blockchain

## Key Features

### Interactive Tray Grid
NIMS displays a visual 72-cell germination tray (12 columns × 6 rows) that mirrors a real nursery tray. Each cell is color-coded:
- Gray/Dark: Empty — tap to plant a seed
- Amber/Yellow: Seed planted, waiting for germination
- Green: Germinated — an NFT is automatically assigned
- Red: Plant died — NFT is burned
- Blue: Transplanted to a larger container

### Container Tracking
Track plants across container sizes:
- 16oz solo cups (seedlings)
- 1 gallon nursery pots
- 5 gallon grow bags
- In-ground planting
- Raised beds
- Custom containers

When you transplant, all data follows the plant. The NFT metadata updates with each transition.

### One-Tap Lifecycle Logging
Every action is designed for quick, one-handed mobile entry:
- 💧 Water: amount, method (hose/drip/rain/misting), pH level
- 🧪 Feed: product name, nutrient type, dilution ratio, amount per plant
- 🐛 Pest/Disease: pest identification, severity, treatment applied, photo evidence
- 📸 Photos: progress photos attached to individual plants
- 📝 Notes: free-form observations

### Weather Integration
NIMS integrates with Open-Meteo (free weather API) to automatically capture:
- Temperature (current, high, low, feels-like/heat index)
- Humidity
- UV Index with color-coded severity
- Rainfall (daily total)
- Wind speed and direction
- Barometric pressure
- Air Quality Index (AQI)
- Moon phase and illumination

Weather data is automatically attached to every lifecycle entry, creating rich provenance data.

### Planting Almanac
The expanded weather panel shows "What to Plant This Month" — zone-specific planting recommendations based on UF/IFAS Extension guidelines for South Florida (Zone 10a). Recommendations include what to start indoors, direct sow, transplant, or harvest.

### Seed Bank
Catalog your entire seed collection:
- Track seed lots with variety, source, quantity, and germination rates
- Record breeding crosses (mother × father) with generation tracking (F1, F2, F3)
- Track seed vendors and sources
- Harvest seeds from tracked plants — auto-links to parent plant data
- Plant from a seed lot — pre-selects the variety in the tray grid

### Variety Management
Build your own variety catalog:
- Add pepper varieties with species, Scoville range, days to germination/maturity
- Search and filter your variety database
- Create new varieties inline while planting seeds

### NFC Plant Tags
Each plant has a unique URL that can be written to an NFC tag. Tap the tag with your phone to instantly view the plant's full lifecycle page. QR codes are also available for printing on pot labels.

## How NIMS Powers NFT Provenance

1. Admin plants a seed in a 72-cell tray in NIMS
2. When the seed germinates, an ICRC-7 NFT is automatically assigned from IC SPICY's 8,888-token collection
3. Every watering, feeding, pest treatment, photo, and weather snapshot is recorded on-chain
4. When the plant is listed for sale, customers can view its complete lifecycle history
5. When purchased, the NFT transfers to the buyer's wallet
6. The buyer adds the plant to their personal NIMS and continues the lifecycle record
7. The NFT becomes a living document — a verifiable certificate of the plant's entire journey
