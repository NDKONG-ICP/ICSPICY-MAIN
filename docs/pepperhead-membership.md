# PepperHead Membership

PepperHeads are 888 membership-bearing NFTs within the 8,888-token IC SPICY collection. They give early supporters deeper access to the brand, community perks, and premium app features — without being tied to a physical plant purchase.

## What PepperHeads Are

Of the 8,888 IC SPICY NFTs, exactly 888 are designated PepperHeads: 50 Founder-tier tokens (token IDs 7839–7888) and 838 Rare-tier tokens (token IDs 7889–8726). Each carries on-chain metadata marking it as a PepperHead membership NFT. They are collectible membership passes, not equity or profit-sharing instruments.

## Benefits

PepperHead holders receive:

- **Shop discounts** — reduced pricing on plant purchases and merchandise (tier-dependent)
- **Premium SpicyAI** — higher daily chat limits and priority access to the knowledge-base chatbot
- **Future drop whitelist** — early access windows for new NFT releases and product launches
- **Community status** — member-first updates, events, and OHSHII governance links
- **DAO access** — connect to OHSHII governance at ohshii.com for SPICY token voting (IC SPICY links out; governance lives on OHSHII, not inside the app)

PepperHead NFTs cannot be burned for SPICY token redemption — they confer ongoing membership utility instead.

## How to Get One

PepperHeads can be purchased as a standalone digital membership (planned fixed-price flow via ICP, ckBTC, or card) or acquired on secondary markets from existing holders. Check the Shop or NFT section for current availability — the app displays how many of the 888 remain unsold.

## What They Are Not

PepperHeads are not securities, equity stakes, guaranteed profit instruments, or claims on treasury or nursery assets. Benefits are product and community utility within the IC SPICY ecosystem. For token economics details, refer to the SPICY utility explainer and token disclosure documents.
