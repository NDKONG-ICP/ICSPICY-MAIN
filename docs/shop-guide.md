# IC SPICY Shop Guide

The IC SPICY shop at https://icspicy.app lets you browse and purchase live pepper plants grown at our FDACS-registered nursery in Port Charlotte, Florida. Each plant listed for sale includes its NIMS lifecycle history and an associated ICRC-7 NFT that transfers to your wallet on purchase.

## Browsing and Buying

Browse available plants from the Shop tab. Each listing shows variety, container size, price, and provenance summary. Tap a plant to view its full lifecycle — watering logs, feeding records, photos, and weather snapshots recorded while it grew at the nursery.

When you're ready to buy, add the plant to your cart and proceed to checkout. You'll need to be signed in with Internet Identity. For crypto payments, connect your OISY or Plug wallet first.

## Payment Options

IC SPICY supports multiple payment paths:

- **ICP and ckBTC** — pay directly from your connected wallet via ICRC-2 approval
- **Stripe** — credit/debit card checkout for customers who prefer traditional payment
- **ICPay** — streamlined crypto checkout for Internet Computer assets
- **SPICY token** — planned utility-token payment with burn mechanics (when live)

Prices are computed server-side at checkout. The frontend displays estimates, but the backend recalculates totals including any PepperHead or SPICY-holder discounts before confirming payment.

## QR Claims (In-Person Sales)

For farmers-market and in-person sales, IC SPICY generates QR claim codes tied to a specific plant NFT. Scan the code while signed in to claim the NFT to your wallet without going through the online checkout flow. The plant is then added to your personal NIMS garden with full nursery provenance intact.

## Shipping

Live plants ship from Port Charlotte, FL. Shipping availability and costs depend on destination and season. Checkout displays applicable shipping options before payment. Plants are packed for transit following nursery best practices; tracking information is provided when available.

## After Purchase

Once payment confirms, the plant NFT transfers to your wallet. NIMS prompts you to adopt the plant into your personal garden so you can continue logging care. The nursery's full history remains visible — your entries append to the same on-chain provenance record.
