IC SPICY — Florida Hot Pepper Nursery with NFT Provenance

IC SPICY is an FDACS-registered nursery in Port Charlotte, Florida, growing the world's rarest and hottest chili peppers — Carolina Reaper, 7 Pot Primo, Scotch Bonnet, and hundreds more — using regenerative Korean Natural Farming (KNF) and JADAM methods with over 30 years of growing experience. Every live plant ships with an NFT on the Internet Computer that proves its provenance and records its full lifecycle from seed to harvest. The site also offers NIMS, a free plant-tracking app with automatic weather capture; a Natural Farming CookBook with 50+ free KNF and JADAM recipes like FPJ, LAB, OHN, and IMO; and a free 3D garden designer with 385 Florida-friendly plants.
