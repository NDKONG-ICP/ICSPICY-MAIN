FAQ

II · OISY · HashPack

How do I sign in?
Use Internet Identity (passkey). That principal owns your roster and pack credits. Allow the II popup if the browser blocks it.
How do I pay with ICP?
Connect OISY, pick Warden or Captain, approve the live ≈ ICP quote, then claim the pack after the transfer confirms.
How do I pay with HBAR?
Connect HashPack, pay the live ≈ HBAR quote, then claim. Wrong network or aborted tx = no credit until a successful transfer + claim.
USD / card?
Stripe checkout at stripe.bonsaios.app — packs credit to the II principal you signed in with.
How do I play after buying?
Open Arena → Collection shows owned cards. Enter Story/Siege/Ranked, prep formation (bench ↔ dock), then Fight to resolve.
